    4.5Microns B-Grade BOPET Film: Cost-Effective TTR & Lamination Base

    4.5Microns BOPET Film for printing & laminating


    4.5Mic B-Grade BOPET films, which are a strategic choice for cost-sensitive applications where the extreme precision of A-grade material isn't strictly necessary, the 4.5 Micron B-Grade BOPET for printing and lamination, specifically tailored for the TTR market.


    While maintaining the core physical properties of polyester, our B-Grade series is optimized for high-volume production in the Barcode and Fax ribbon industries. This grade offers a balance between industrial-grade performance and economic efficiency.

    Quality Profile: B-Grade vs. Standard

    B-Grade film typically features the same thermal resistance as A-grade but may have minor variations that do not impact the final transfer quality in standard applications:

    • Surface Consistency: Minor surface visual variations that do not affect ink adhesion or back-coating.
    • Splice Count: May contain a slightly higher frequency of factory joins per jumbo roll (clearly marked for converters).
    • Thickness Tolerance: Slight variations within the +/- 3-5% range, still suitable for most barcode and fax thermal heads.

    Technical Specification Table

    Key Applications

    1. Barcode Ribbons (Wax & Wax/Resin): The 4.5um B-grade is the industry standard for shipping labels and retail tags where high-speed throughput and cost-per-print are key.
    2. Fax Thermal Ribbons: Excellent heat dissipation ensures the film does not stick to the thermal print head during long transmission cycles.
    3. Lamination Base: Used as a thin carrier layer in multi-layer flexible packaging or protective laminates for paper-based tags.
    4. Textile Transfer: Suitable for heat-press applications where graphics are transferred from the BOPET carrier onto fabrics.

    Processing Benefits for Converters

    • High Heat Resistance: Even in B-grade, the BOPET backbone prevents melting at standard thermal print temperatures (200degree
    • + peaks).
    • Dimensional Stability: Ensures that barcode "bars" and QR codes remain perfectly crisp and do not distort during the heat-transfer process.
    • Slitting Compatibility: Designed to be slit into narrow widths (e.g., 55mm, 110mm) without edge fraying or cracking.
    • 4.5 Microns BOPET Film clear for metallizes Bopet polyester metallized film has a super reflective, mirror- like surface. It is a oriented polyester film with a very
    • thin coating of aluminum that is vacuum deposited on to the surface of the film. This film is used in a variety of
    • industrial and applications, including printing, packaging, crafts and many other applications.
    • Main features of bopet polyester clear film
    • a. Good Metal Bond Strength
    • b. Good Barrier properties of oxygen, light, water vapor
    • c. Good Lamination Bond Strength
    • d. Excellent Machinability
    • thickness of BOPET film:4.4,4.5,4.55,4.6,4.8,5.0,5.7,6.0um
      width of BOPET basic film: 500,600,910,980,1010,1200,1500mm
      length of BOPET basic film:15000,17000,20000,24000,37000m
      feature of BOPET basic film: clear/transparent

    Note on Storage: To maintain B-grade integrity, rolls should be kept in their original polyethylene wrapping until use to prevent edge damage or dust accumulation, which can cause "pinholes" in the final ribbon coating.
